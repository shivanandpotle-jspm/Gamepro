How to Play

Rules, Scoring Mechanisms, and Mastery Strategies

1

Flip & Recall

Tap or click any face-down card to reveal its symbol. Then flip a second card. If both match, they stay face-up and you earn points!

2

Keep The Streak Burning

Consecutive matches build a Streak Multiplier (x1.25, x1.5, x2.0+). Each matching pair in a streak yields exponentially higher points!

3

Speed & Efficiency

Completing the board faster than the target time awards massive Speed Bonuses. Minimizing unnecessary moves boosts your move efficiency score.

4

Strategic Power-ups

Use Hint to briefly illuminate a hidden matching pair when stuck (-25 pts). Use Shuffle once per match to reshuffle the remaining cards.

Scoring Breakdown Formula

$$\text{Final Score} = \left(\text{Base Match Points} + \text{Speed Bonus} + \text{Efficiency Bonus} + \text{Streak Bonus} - \text{Hint Penalty}\right) \times \text{Difficulty Multiplier}$$

• Easy: 1.0x Multiplier (Target: 45s)
• Medium: 1.5x Multiplier (Target: 60s)
• Hard: 2.0x Multiplier (Target: 90s)
• Expert: 2.5x Multiplier (Target: 120s)
